FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What types of projects does Anchor Point work on?

Anchor Point provides professional engineering, project management and infrastructure advisory services across relevant road, building and civil infrastructure assignments. The scope of each engagement is determined by the client’s requirements and the technical capabilities required.

Which organizations do you work with?

We work with public institutions, government agencies, private developers, contractors, development partners and other organizations involved in infrastructure development and management.

Can Anchor Point provide independent construction supervision?

Yes. We offer construction supervision and technical oversight services within our qualified areas of practice, supporting clients in monitoring implementation against approved designs, specifications and contractual requirements.

Can you join a project that is already under construction?

Yes. Subject to the project requirements, we can provide project review, technical assessment, monitoring, construction oversight and advisory support for ongoing assignments.

What is your approach to project management?

Our approach combines structured planning, continuous monitoring, coordination and proactive risk management to support effective project implementation and informed decision-making.

How does Anchor Point use AI and digital technology?

We integrate modern digital tools and AI-assisted analysis into relevant engineering and project management workflows to improve document review, project information analysis, monitoring and reporting. Professional engineers remain responsible for the review and interpretation of relevant outputs.

How do you protect confidential project information?

We treat client project information as confidential and handle it in accordance with applicable contractual obligations and our information-management procedures. Specific data handling arrangements can be agreed as part of an engagement.

Can you provide a proposal for a project?

Yes. Contact us with an outline of your project, location, scope and requirements. Our team can review the information and determine the appropriate next steps.

Where does Anchor Point operate?

Anchor Point is based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, and serves clients across Ethiopia, subject to the scope and requirements of each assignment.

How can we contact your team?

You can reach us through the contact details on our website or submit a project inquiry through our online contact form.

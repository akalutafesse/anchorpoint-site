Featured Professional Capability

Advancing a performance-based approach to road maintenance.

Traditional infrastructure maintenance arrangements can place greater emphasis on completed activities and measured quantities than on the long-term performance of the assets being maintained.

Performance-based maintenance approaches offer a different model, linking defined service levels, measurable performance indicators and structured inspection processes to maintenance obligations.

Professional experience in this area includes involvement in the preparation of an OPRC proposal for the Ethiopian Roads Administration, alongside work related to infrastructure monitoring, asset management and maintenance systems.

Establish measurable road service standards.
Define maintenance obligations.
Monitor infrastructure condition.
Verify performance through inspections.
Link payment to contractual performance requirements.

About Us

Built on experience. Looking ahead.

An Ethiopian engineering and infrastructure consultancy.

Engineers who know how projects are really built.

Anchor Point Engineers and Consultants provides technical services, project management and infrastructure advisory.

We work with public institutions, private developers, contractors and development partners. Our advice is technically sound, commercially informed and shaped by each client’s needs.

Our mission

To provide dependable engineering and infrastructure advisory services that strengthen project delivery, improve infrastructure management and create lasting value for our clients and communities.

Our vision

To become a respected engineering and infrastructure consultancy, recognized for professional excellence, technical innovation and meaningful contributions to infrastructure development in Ethiopia and beyond.

Five things we hold to.

Integrity

Honest, transparent work that respects our professional and contractual obligations.

Technical Excellence

Sound engineering, careful analysis and professional standards.

Accountability

We take responsibility for the quality and accuracy of our work.

Collaboration

We work closely with clients and specialist partners.

Innovation

Modern tools and better methods for planning, building and managing infrastructure.

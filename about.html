Who We Are

Who We Are

Anchor Point Engineers and Consultants is an Ethiopian engineering and infrastructure consultancy established to provide professional technical services, project management and infrastructure advisory solutions.

We support public institutions, private developers, contractors and development partners in addressing the technical and management challenges associated with infrastructure development.

Our work is guided by a practical understanding of infrastructure implementation, professional engineering principles and a commitment to delivering solutions that are technically sound, commercially informed and responsive to client requirements.

Our Mission

To provide dependable engineering and infrastructure advisory services that strengthen project delivery, improve infrastructure management and create lasting value for our clients and communities.

Our Vision

To become a respected engineering and infrastructure consultancy recognized for professional excellence, technical innovation and meaningful contributions to infrastructure development in Ethiopia and beyond.

Our Values

Our Values

Integrity

We conduct our work with honesty, transparency and respect for professional and contractual obligations.

Technical Excellence

We apply sound engineering principles, rigorous analysis and professional standards to every assignment.

Accountability

We take responsibility for the quality, accuracy and professional integrity of our work.

Collaboration

We work closely with clients, project stakeholders and specialist partners to achieve shared objectives.

Innovation

We embrace modern tools, technology and improved methodologies to strengthen the way infrastructure is planned, delivered and managed.

Our Approach

Understanding before execution. Discipline throughout delivery.

Every project has different technical requirements, constraints and objectives. We begin by understanding the client’s needs, the project context and the factors that influence successful implementation.

We then apply a structured approach to planning, technical assessment, execution oversight and performance monitoring.

Our objective is to provide clear professional advice, practical solutions and reliable support at every stage of the engagement.

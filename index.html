Who We Are

A practical understanding of infrastructure. A commitment to better outcomes.

Anchor Point Engineers and Consultants is an Ethiopian engineering and infrastructure consultancy established to provide professional technical services, project management and infrastructure advisory solutions.

We work across the infrastructure lifecycle, supporting clients from early-stage planning and technical assessment through project implementation, construction supervision and long-term asset management.

Our approach combines experienced engineering professionals, disciplined project management and modern analytical capabilities.

We believe that successful infrastructure delivery requires more than technical knowledge. It requires sound judgment, accountability, effective coordination and a clear understanding of the objectives each project is intended to achieve.

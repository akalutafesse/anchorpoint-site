Eng. Biniyam Regassa Merga

Founder & Chief Executive Officer M.Sc. Civil Engineering Practicing Professional Engineer
Discuss a Project

Eng. Biniyam Regassa Merga is an infrastructure and engineering professional with more than 15 years of experience spanning road infrastructure development, project management and road asset management in Ethiopia.

During his career at the Ethiopian Roads Administration, he progressed from Project Engineer to senior responsibilities within Road Asset Management, including serving in an acting road maintenance director capacity.

His professional experience encompasses involvement in major road construction programmes, infrastructure maintenance systems, performance-based road maintenance initiatives, institutional monitoring frameworks and the application of digital technologies to infrastructure management.

He brings this combination of public-sector experience, technical knowledge and project delivery expertise to Anchor Point, with a commitment to strengthening infrastructure outcomes through professional accountability, disciplined execution and modern engineering practices.

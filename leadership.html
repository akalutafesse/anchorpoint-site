Eng. Biniyam Regassa Merga is an infrastructure and engineering professional with more than 15 years of experience in road development, project management and road asset management in Ethiopia.

During his career at the Ethiopian Roads Administration, he progressed from Project Engineer to senior responsibilities within Road Asset Management, including serving in an acting road maintenance director capacity.

His experience includes major road construction programmes, maintenance systems, performance-based road maintenance, monitoring frameworks and the use of digital technology in infrastructure management.

He brings this public-sector experience and technical knowledge to Anchor Point.
